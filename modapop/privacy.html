Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy applies to ModaPop on iPhone and iPad, provided by JOYBOOST LIMITED (JoyBoost). It does not replace the separate Selah Duo: Bible Reader policy.

1. Information we process

ModaPop does not require a JoyBoost account. Outfits, progress and preferences are saved on your device. Depending on consent and features used, the game and its service providers process gameplay events (such as screen visits, round results and reward completion); app and operating-system versions; device model, language, IP address and approximate region; installation identifiers; crash diagnostics; and advertising impressions, clicks, placement and revenue information. Advertising identifiers are accessed only where permitted.

Apple processes App Store payments. Product identifiers, receipts, transaction status and purchase entitlements are used to deliver and restore purchases; JoyBoost does not receive your full payment-card information. When you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

2. Why we use information

We use information to run the game, save progress, deliver purchased entitlements and rewards, respond to support requests, diagnose crashes, improve reliability and gameplay, serve and measure advertising, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, processing relies on providing requested services, legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the service, legal obligations or consent as applicable. We request consent where required and provide the controls described below.

3. Service providers and advertising partners

ModaPop integrates Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config, Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform. Ad mediation includes Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Meta Audience Network, ironSource, InMobi, Chartboost and Pangle. Actual processing depends on the active ad request, region, consent and provider configuration; inclusion of an SDK does not mean every provider receives information in every session. Partners may process device, network and advertising information to deliver, measure, personalize where permitted, and secure their services.

Read the providers' notices: Google, Firebase, Unity and ironSource, Liftoff, Meta, InMobi, Chartboost, Pangle and Apple.

4. iOS permissions and advertising choices

App Tracking Transparency (ATT): iOS may ask whether ModaPop can track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Declining does not block core gameplay or require a purchase. You can change the choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. ATT and regional advertising consent are separate choices. Where available, open the game's Settings > Privacy Options to review or withdraw regional consent. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads or all processing used to deliver and protect them.

Notifications: with permission, ModaPop schedules optional reminders locally on your device. This feature does not use a remote push service. Manage notifications in iOS Settings > Notifications > ModaPop. Current game features do not request access to your camera, microphone, location or photo library.

5. Access, deletion and other rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of targeted advertising or sharing as defined by applicable law. Contact joyboost.uk@gmail.com with the app name, platform and your request. We may ask for proportionate information to locate records and verify the request, and respond as required by applicable law. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

Deleting the app can remove local progress and settings, but does not automatically delete information retained by providers. Email us for help with those requests. ModaPop has no JoyBoost account to delete. Purchase restoration is separate from local gameplay progress.

6. Retention, security and international transfers

We keep information only as needed to provide the service, resolve requests, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention varies by data type and the provider settings in use. Support correspondence is retained only for resolving the request and necessary follow-up or legal obligations. Providers may process data in countries other than yours; applicable safeguards are used where required. We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

7. Children and changes

The store's age rating describes content suitability and is separate from consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect children's information in violation of applicable law. Parents or guardians can contact us about suspected collection so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable. We update this policy as practices change and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.

8. Website and contact

Our static website's hosting and font services are described in the website privacy notice. For ModaPop privacy or support requests, contact JOYBOOST LIMITED (JoyBoost) at joyboost.uk@gmail.com.
