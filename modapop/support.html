Support

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Need help with ModaPop? Email joyboost.uk@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are helpful; hide personal or payment information first.

Restore Remove Ads

Use Home > Remove Ads > Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. This restores eligible purchase ownership, not outfits or game progress removed from the device. If it does not work, check your connection and contact us with the product and transaction reference. Do not purchase again just to restore an existing entitlement.

Purchases and refunds

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Review ModaPop's terms for details. Refund requests can be made through Apple's Report a Problem service.

Privacy and permissions

Read the ModaPop Privacy Policy. Use the game's Privacy Options where available for regional consent, iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking for ATT, and iOS Settings > Notifications > ModaPop for local reminders. Email us for data access or deletion requests.

Game progress

ModaPop saves progress locally and does not require a JoyBoost account. Removing the app or device data may remove progress. Contact us before reinstalling if you are trying to resolve a problem.
