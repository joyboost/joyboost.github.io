Privacy Policy

Last updated / 更新日期: 8 November 2026

This is the shared privacy policy for JOYBOOST LIMITED (JoyBoost), our website and the mobile apps that link to this page. It covers iOS/iPadOS and Android. The details below explain which features and data practices apply to each app.
这是 JoyBoost 官网及引用本页的 iOS、iPadOS、Android 应用统一隐私政策。不同应用的数据处理以本页对应说明为准。

1. Who we are and how to contact us

JOYBOOST LIMITED (JoyBoost) provides these services. For privacy questions, support or data requests, email joyboost.uk@gmail.com. Include the app name, platform and app version so we can identify the service involved. Do not send passwords, private notes or full payment-card information.

2. Games: gameplay, diagnostics and advertising

Our dress-up game is presented as ModaPop on iPhone and iPad and may be listed as Left or Right on Android. It does not require a JoyBoost account. Outfits, progress and preferences are saved in local app storage. Device backups may include local data according to your system settings; purchase restoration is separate from restoring gameplay progress.

Depending on the platform, consent and features used, the game and its providers process gameplay events such as screen visits, round results and reward completion; app and operating-system versions; device model and language; IP address and approximate region; installation identifiers; crash diagnostics; and ad impressions, clicks, placements and revenue information. Advertising identifiers, including Apple's IDFA or Android's advertising ID, are accessed only where permitted. An approximate region derived from an IP address is different from permission to access precise device location.

On Android, where Google Play Games is available and you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile and achievements. The game uses a player identifier and achievement state to associate and report unlocked achievements. Google Play Games settings control that service. It is not a separate JoyBoost account, and core gameplay remains available if sign-in is unavailable.

3. Reading apps: local data and optional features

Selah Duo: Bible Reader has no JoyBoost account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated server. JoyBoost does not collect reading history, bookmarks, notes or identifiers from the app, and it does not track you across apps or websites. Reading positions, annotations, note drafts, plans, preferences, reminder schedules and rating-prompt timing stay in local app storage. There is no app-managed cloud sync; system backups may include this data according to your device settings.

Read-aloud uses system speech synthesis and does not record audio or request microphone access. The operating system manages available voices and voice downloads. Android speech engines may use online services according to their settings and providers' policies; fully offline speech requires an installed offline voice. Sharing and backup export occur only when you choose them. An exported backup contains your personal reading data and notes; a destination app, Files or cloud service handles the copy under its own policy. Import reads only the file you select.

Selah Duo: Bible Reader is a paid download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The advertising and game analytics described in section 2 do not apply to this reading app.

4. Purchases and support information

Apple App Store or Google Play handles downloads, payments, refunds and store reviews under its own policies. In games offering in-app purchases, product identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, transaction status and entitlements are used to deliver, verify and restore purchases. ModaPop's Remove Ads option is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. We do not receive your full payment-card information. Opening store, support or privacy links connects to external services that may receive normal web connection information.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond, resolve the request and conduct necessary follow-up. Our email provider processes correspondence under its own policy.

5. Why we process information

We use information relevant to the app to operate requested features, save local progress, deliver purchases and rewards, provide support, diagnose crashes, improve reliability and gameplay, serve and measure advertising where included, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, processing relies on providing requested services, legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the service, legal obligations or consent, as applicable. We request consent where required. App-store content age ratings are separate from consent to data processing.

6. Service providers and disclosures

Our ad-supported game integrates Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config, Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Mediation partners include Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Meta Audience Network, ironSource, InMobi, Chartboost and Pangle. Processing depends on the active feature or ad request, platform, region, consent and provider configuration; not every provider receives information in every session. Providers may process device, network and ad information to deliver, measure, personalize where permitted, and secure their services. These advertising and analytics integrations do not apply to Selah Duo: Bible Reader.

Read the providers' notices: Google and Google Play, Firebase, Unity and ironSource, Liftoff, Meta, InMobi, Chartboost, Pangle and Apple.

We may disclose information when necessary to comply with law, protect users and the service, or complete a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards and notice. Data shared with advertising providers may be treated as targeted advertising or sharing under some privacy laws; the choices below apply where available or required.

7. Advertising choices and platform permissions

Regional consent: in ad-supported games, Google's User Messaging Platform requests consent or offers other choices where required. Where available, use the game's Settings > Privacy Options to review or withdraw your choice. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads or processing needed to deliver and protect them.

iOS/iPadOS tracking: App Tracking Transparency (ATT) asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites where required. Declining does not block core gameplay or require a purchase. Manage this choice in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. ATT and regional advertising consent are separate choices.

Android advertising: use your device's privacy or Ads settings to manage or delete the advertising ID and available advertising controls. Paths vary by Android version and manufacturer. These controls are separate from in-app regional consent.

Notifications: supported apps schedule optional reminders locally on your device after permission where required. This reminder feature does not use a remote push service. Manage reminders in the app where offered, or in iOS/iPadOS Settings > Notifications or Android Settings > Apps > the app > Notifications. Battery-saving settings may delay reminders. Current features of the apps described here do not request camera, microphone, precise location or photo-library access. File selection and sharing in the reading app are initiated by you through system controls.

8. Access, deletion and your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of targeted advertising or sharing as defined by applicable law. Email joyboost.uk@gmail.com with the app name, platform and request. We may ask for proportionate information to locate records and verify a request, and respond within applicable legal time limits. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Deleting an app generally removes its local app data. Android also offers a clear-storage control. Export reading backups first if you wish to retain notes. Exported copies, system backups, store purchase records and Play Games data must be managed separately through their services. Uninstalling does not automatically erase information retained by advertising or analytics providers; contact us for assistance with those requests. The apps described here have no JoyBoost account to delete.

9. Retention, security and international processing

Local data remains on your device until you remove it, reset app storage or uninstall, subject to system backup settings. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve requests and necessary follow-up or legal obligations. Other providers' retention depends on the data type, their settings and legal obligations. We keep information only as necessary to provide the relevant service, resolve requests, prevent fraud and meet legal requirements.

Service providers may process information outside your country; applicable transfer safeguards are used where required. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect children's information in violation of applicable law. Where consent from a parent or guardian is required, it must be obtained before the relevant processing. Parents or guardians can contact us about suspected collection so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

11. This website

This static website has no account registration, contact form, advertising scripts or analytics scripts. It is hosted through GitHub Pages, whose provider may process connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. Some pages load Google Fonts and connect to Google. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

12. Changes

We update this policy when our practices change and revise the date above. We provide additional notice or seek consent where required. Existing app-specific privacy URLs remain accessible and direct to this unified policy.
