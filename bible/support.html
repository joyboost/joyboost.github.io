Reading offline · 离线阅读

Scripture is included in the app. System read-aloud depends on installed voices; Android speech engines may use the internet according to their settings. Install an offline voice for fully offline speech.
经文随应用提供。朗读依赖系统声音；Android 语音引擎可能联网，完全离线朗读需要安装离线声音。

Notes and backups · 笔记与备份

Your reading data stays in app storage. Export a backup from Settings before replacing your device or uninstalling. Use the backup import option to restore the file you select. Exported files and device backups are managed separately; the app does not provide automatic cloud sync.
阅读数据保存在应用内。换机或卸载前请在设置中导出备份，之后选择文件导入恢复。导出文件和系统备份需自行管理，应用不提供自动云同步。

Reminders · 阅读提醒

Enable reminders in the app and allow notifications in system settings. Battery-saving modes may delay delivery.
请在应用中开启提醒，并在系统设置中允许通知；省电模式可能延迟提醒。
