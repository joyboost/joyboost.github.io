Selah Plus and free trials · 订阅与免费试用

On supported iOS/iPadOS editions, open Ad-free to view Selah Plus. Annual and monthly plans remove app-open, interstitial, banner and rewarded ads while membership is active. Core reading is available without subscribing. The purchase screen shows your local price, billing period and any eligible trial. Trial eligibility is determined by Apple; previous use of an introductory offer in the same subscription group can affect eligibility. Switching plans does not guarantee another trial.
在支持的 iOS/iPadOS 版中，点击“免广告”查看年订阅和月订阅。会员有效期内移除各类广告，不订阅也能阅读。实际价格、周期和试用资格以购买页和 Apple 确认页为准；切换套餐不保证再次获得试用。

Cancel or change a subscription · 取消或更改订阅

Use Manage subscriptions in Selah Plus, or iPhone/iPad Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Selah. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. For a free or discounted trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends if you do not want to renew. Apple displays your access end date; cancelling a trial may end access immediately. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. See Apple's cancellation instructions.
通过会员页“管理订阅”，或系统“设置 > 你的姓名 > 订阅”管理。订阅会自动续费；如不想续费，请至少在试用结束前 24 小时取消。有效期以 Apple 显示为准，取消试用可能立即失去权益。卸载应用不会取消订阅。

Restore purchases · 恢复购买

Connect to the internet and sign in to the Apple Account used for the purchase, then open Ad-free > Restore purchases. An active eligible subscription should restore without buying again. This restores membership, not reading notes or backups. An older paid app download is a separate store purchase, not a Selah Plus subscription; contact us with the version and purchase reference if you need help. If an active subscription still shows ads, reopen the app, restore and contact us. Do not pay again just to troubleshoot.
联网并使用购买时的 Apple 账号，在“免广告”页选择“恢复购买”。恢复的是有效订阅，不是笔记或备份。旧版付费下载与 Plus 订阅是不同购买；如有疑问请提供版本和交易参考编号联系我们。有效会员仍见广告时，请重新打开应用并恢复购买，不要为排查问题重复付款。

Ads and privacy choices · 广告与隐私选项

Where required and available, open Ad-free / Selah Plus > Ad privacy options to review regional consent. These choices are separate from Apple tracking settings. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily remove all ads. Selah Plus removes ads but does not by itself disable diagnostics. If offered, completing a rewarded video grants the displayed period without interstitials; this is not a full ad-free subscription. An unavailable ad or an early close does not grant a reward. Core reading remains available.
需要且可用时，在会员页“广告隐私设置”查看地区同意选项；它与 Apple 跟踪权限不同。拒绝个性化广告不一定移除所有广告，会员免广告不等于关闭诊断。激励视频完成后按页面说明暂免插屏，不等于所有广告均移除；广告不可用或提前关闭不会获得奖励，不影响核心阅读。

Reading offline · 离线阅读

Scripture is included in the app. System read-aloud depends on installed voices; Android speech engines may use the internet according to their settings. Install an offline voice for fully offline speech. Purchases, restoration and advertising require a network connection.
经文随应用提供。朗读依赖系统声音；Android 语音引擎可能联网，完全离线朗读需要安装离线声音。购买、恢复购买及广告需要联网。

Notes and backups · 笔记与备份

Your notes and reading data stay in app storage unless you share or export them. Export a backup from Settings before replacing your device or uninstalling. Import the backup file you select to restore it. Exported files and system backups are managed separately; there is no app-managed automatic cloud sync. Contact us before reinstalling to troubleshoot missing data.
笔记及阅读数据保存在本机，除非你主动分享或导出。换机或卸载前请在设置中导出备份，并通过选择备份文件导入恢复。导出文件和系统备份需分别管理，应用不提供自动云同步；排查数据丢失时请先联系我们，不要直接卸载。

Reminders · 阅读提醒

Enable reminders in the app and allow notifications in system settings. Battery-saving modes may delay delivery. Reminders are scheduled locally.
请在应用中开启提醒，并在系统设置中允许通知；提醒在本机安排，省电模式可能延迟提醒。

Billing and refunds · 账单与退款

Apple or Google Play handles payments and refund eligibility for purchases through that store. For Apple purchases, use Report a Problem or Apple billing support. For existing Google Play purchases, see Google Play refunds. We cannot guarantee a refund or cancel a store subscription on your behalf.
通过哪家商店购买，就由该商店处理付款及退款资格。我们无法保证退款或代你取消商店订阅。
