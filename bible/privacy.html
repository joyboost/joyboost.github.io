Selah Duo: Bible Reader keeps your reading personal. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or developer-operated server. We do not collect your reading history, bookmarks, notes or identifiers, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Reading positions, annotations, note drafts, plans and preferences are stored in the app’s local storage. Reminder schedules and rating-prompt timing are also stored locally. iOS / Android device backups may include this data according to your system settings. There is no app-managed cloud sync.

Notifications are optional local reading reminders, scheduled on your device after you allow notifications. You can turn them off in the app or iOS / Android Settings. Read-aloud uses system speech synthesis; available voices and any voice downloads are managed by the operating system. The app does not record audio or request microphone access. On Android, speech engines may use online services according to their settings; voice downloads and online speech are governed by the provider’s policy. Fully offline speech requires a suitable installed offline voice.

Sharing and backup export happen only when you choose them. A backup contains your personal reading data and notes. If you choose another app, Files or a cloud destination in the system share sheet, that destination handles the copy under its own privacy policy. Import reads only the backup file you select.

Apple App Store or Google Play handles purchases and reviews under the relevant Apple or Google privacy policy. Selah Duo: Bible Reader is a paid download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not receive payment-card details. Opening support, privacy or store links visits external services that may receive normal web connection information. No purchase account or review content is uploaded by the app to JoyBoost.

You can remove saved passages and notes in Collections, and disable reminders in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; exported copies and system backups must be managed separately. Export a backup first if you wish to retain your notes. There is no server-side account or data to delete.

JoyBoost provides Selah Duo: Bible Reader. For privacy or support, email joyboost.uk@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, use it to respond and resolve your request, and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Our email provider processes correspondence under its own policy. Avoid sending private notes or payment details. This policy covers iOS and Android version 1.0.0 and will be updated if data practices change.
